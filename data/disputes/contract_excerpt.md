## §4 Complaint window

Buyer must notify Seller of quantity, quality, or price disputes in writing within
**30 calendar days** of delivery (DESADV/DDT date). Claims after the window may be
rejected unless Seller agrees in writing.

## §7 Delivery tolerance

- Quantity tolerance: **±2%** of ordered cartons/eaches, or **2 units**, whichever
  is greater, without penalty when DESADV and signed DDT agree.
- Short shipments notified in DESADV and matched on the invoice are not Buyer
  receiving errors; Seller remains responsible for commercial follow-up (re-ship or
  credit).
- Delivery slot (OTIF): arrivals outside the booked warehouse window are OTIF misses;
  liquidated damages apply **only if** an amount is stated in the retailer’s slot
  annex (none fixed in this excerpt — do not invent a penalty €).

## §15 Credits

Accepted disputes are settled by **credit note** (FatturaPA TD04 / EDI INVOIC 381),
never by issuing a replacement invoice for the same delivery.
